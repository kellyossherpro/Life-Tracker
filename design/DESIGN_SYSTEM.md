# Life Index — Design System

Extracted from Kelly's inspiration boards (light + dark "Galaxy Night" versions).

## Overall vibe
**Dreamy, Sparkly, Organized & Empowering.**
"Like a magical planner bestie in your pocket that keeps your life beautifully in order."

Design philosophy: Magical yet modern · 3D soft elements & depth · bright but calm colours ·
organized & empowering · rounded everything · soft shadows · sparkles & magic details ·
fun but elegant · encouraging & personal.

## Typography
- **Playfair Display** — headers & titles (the "Life Index" logo, screen headings)
- **Poppins** — body text & UI
- **Dancing Script** — accents & personal touch (greetings, flourishes)

## Gradient styles
Aurora Glow · Cotton Candy · Sunset Dream · Ocean Breeze · Galaxy Night

## Textures / backgrounds
Starry Night · Soft Clouds · Pearl Holographic · Glitter Dust · Marble Swirl

## Icon style
3D & soft, rounded, glossy — house, shield, chef bowl, wallet, calendar, scale, heart,
sprout, bell, folder, bag, gear, water drop, flame.

## UI elements
- Frosted/translucent cards, generous rounded corners, soft shadows with depth
- Primary buttons: gradient (purple→pink) pill, with ✨
- Secondary buttons: outlined / ghost
- Bottom navigation: Home · Vault · ➕ (center FAB) · Food/Money · More
- Sparkle accents used tastefully, not on every element

## Screens seen in mockups
- **Home** — "Good morning, Kelly ✨", Today's Snapshot (rent, medical aid, period, spending),
  Quick Add (Add Expense · Add Food · Log Weight · Add Reminder)
- **Foods & Recipes** — recipe card w/ photo, time · cost/serving · servings, ingredient list
  with prices, total cost, "Cook This"
- **Spending Overview** — total spent, category donut, recent transactions
- **Widgets** — Weight Tracker, Period Forecast, Water Intake, Habit Streak, Upcoming Reminders

## Build note
Kelly's direction: **navigable separate pages, NOT one cluttered scroll.** Keep each screen
focused and calm; sparkle is delight, not noise. Sensitive vault text stays high-contrast.
