How it actually works

Three pieces, and only one of them costs a cent — zero cents

Everything you do saves instantly on your phone, even with no signal. When you're online, a copy syncs to a free cloud so it's safely backed up. The sensitive stuff is locked before it ever leaves your phone.

On your phone

The app (PWA)

  • Opens from your home screen, works offline
  • Saves to your phone's own storage first — instant, no waiting
  • Locks vault fields (ID, policy numbers) with a passphrase only you know
encrypted sync ↕ when online

Free private cloud

Backup & sync

  • Your own account, guarded by your login
  • Restores everything onto a new phone in seconds
  • Can't read your locked fields — they arrive already scrambled

The plain-English versionThink of your phone as the notebook you write in, and the cloud as a photocopy kept in a safe. You always work in the notebook; the safe just means you never lose it — and the private pages are in a code even the safe's owner can't crack.

Phase 1 · first to build

Module 1 — Personal Info Vault

Every "where's that detail again?" answered in one place: medical aid, insurance, internet, rent, car, ID numbers, account numbers. Because some of this is genuinely sensitive, the vault has an extra rule — the private fields are encrypted on your phone before syncing, so no server ever sees them in the clear.

categoryMedical Aid · Insurance · Internet · Rent · Car · ID & Docs · Utilities · Subscriptions

On safetyFields marked 🔒 locked are scrambled with a passphrase only you know (built on your phone's own encryption). Even if someone got into the cloud account, those fields would be unreadable. The trade-off: forget the passphrase and those fields can't be recovered — so we'll set that up carefully, together.

Nudges

How reminders reach you

Reminders come from due dates you've already entered — a rent day, a renewal, an expected period, a weigh-in you've skipped. The app schedules them through your phone's own notification system, so they arrive even when the app is closed.

One honest limitationOn iPhone, web-app notifications only work once the app is added to your home screen (iOS 16.4+) — which you're doing anyway. On Android they just work. Either way, this is the free path; a paid App Store version would only be needed if that ever felt too limited.
